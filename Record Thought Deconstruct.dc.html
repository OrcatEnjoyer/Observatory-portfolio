01 · THE BRIEF

Something meaningful in two minutes

A digital experience designed to help users gain perspective on a heavy thought and leave with a meaningful shift in how they see it, all within two minutes.

02 · THE IDEA

Step back from the thought

A heavy thought can feel overwhelming when viewed up close. Through slow breathing exercise, the thought gradually shrinks and is reframed as “I’m having the thought that…”. As everyday moments appear around it, the thought becomes one part of a much bigger picture.
