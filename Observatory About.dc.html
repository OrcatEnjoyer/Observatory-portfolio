OBSERVER PROFILE

Vipatr Kuptasanti

I figure out how something is supposed to work, then build the thing that makes it work better.

That has meant corporate sites, e-commerce storefronts across Lazada and Shopee, a VR simulator, and an interactive installation running on servos and Arduino. The medium keeps changing, but the way I work stays the same. I look at how people move through a system, find what is getting in the way, design a better solution, and make it work. When I can, I build it myself.

Four years at National Taipei University of Technology taught me that interaction design is not limited to screens. Working across design, development and hardware also taught me to look at the whole system instead of focusing on one part of it. I am comfortable working through user flows and design decisions, building digital solutions, solving technical problems, and presenting recommendations to leadership.
