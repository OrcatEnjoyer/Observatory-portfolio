CASE STUDY

SB Chemical Website Redesign

01 · PROBLEM

The original SB Chemical website was cluttered, difficult to navigate, and lacked the professional tone expected in a B2B platform. An audit revealed issues with weak hierarchy, inconsistent product displays, and poor user flow.

  1. 01Website looks outdated and doesn’t match the brand image.
  2. 02Sections of the site lack continuity, which confuses users.
  3. 03Not supported across devices, especially mobile.
  4. 04Cluttered, unsystematic content layout.
  5. 05No clear or compelling CTA, such as “Enquire” or “Order now” buttons.
  6. 06Some pages or sections are unnecessary and confuse users.
02 · APPROACH

I created wireframes for a new homepage that emphasized a product-first layout, clear navigation, and a partner showcase. The design process focused on improving structure while keeping the experience simple and accessible.

03 · OUTCOME

The finalized prototype delivered a modern, responsive interface with multi-language support and smooth performance. Presented directly to the CEO, the redesign aligned the company’s digital presence with its corporate identity and customer needs.
